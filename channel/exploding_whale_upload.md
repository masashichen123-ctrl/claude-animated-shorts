**Title:** Oregon Used Half a Ton of Dynamite on a Dead Whale
(Alternative: The Day It Rained Whale in Oregon)

**Description:**
In 1970, an 8-ton sperm whale washed up near Florence, Oregon. The highway department decided to treat it like a boulder and blow it up with half a ton of dynamite. The plan was for seagulls to eat the pieces. Instead, blubber rained down on the crowd and crushed a car nearly a quarter mile away.

Sources: Oregon Encyclopedia "Florence Whale Explosion"; OPB "Curious Northwest"; KATU.
