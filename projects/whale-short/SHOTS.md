# The Exploding Whale: shot list (30 fps, TOTAL = 1590 f = 53.0 s)

- Voice: `voice/voice.wav` (Kokoro am_michael, 52.4 s).
- Word start frames: `voice/words.json` (seconds × 30).
- Picture and SFX events are locked to the frames below. Visual reactions land 0-3 f before their word.
- XF = 5 f anchored dissolve. Everything else is a hard cut.
- Tone: playful cartoon. Nobody is hurt; blubber lands near people, splats on hats and umbrellas, and they run. The whale is a big cartoon carcass: grey-brown, X-eyes, never gory, no blood or guts. Blubber chunks are pale cream-grey blobs.

Cast (our own designs, toon.ts Pen style as in the emu and bat shorts):
- **The Engineer:** white hard hat, short-sleeve shirt and tie, clipboard, thick glasses; confident and then sooty.
- **Highway crew:** orange vests and hard hats, shovels.
- **Locals:** 1970s clothes (flares, big collars, headscarves); a kid.
- **The TV reporter:** 1970s suit, big tie, handheld mic.
- **The cameraman:** big shoulder film camera.
- **Seagulls:** cute, recurring comic chorus. Poses: stand, fly, faint, wear bibs with forks, flee, wink.
