# The Bat Bomb (1943) - script

## Facts (sources: Wikipedia "Bat bomb"; Task & Purpose "That time the US military burned down one of its own bases with a 'bat bomb'"; Air & Space Forces Magazine "The Bat Bombers"; NPS "Bat Bombs and Balloons on Fire"; Stars and Stripes 2021 "bat bombs New Mexico tests")
- Idea of Lytle S. Adams, a dental surgeon from Pennsylvania, after a visit to Carlsbad Caverns (New Mexico), home to huge colonies of Mexican free-tailed bats.
- Plan: attach small timed incendiaries to bats, release them over Japanese cities in a bomb casing that opens in the air; bats roost in attics and eaves; many Japanese buildings of the time were wood and paper.
- Roosevelt approved the project; it went to the Army Air Forces.
- Louis Fieser, the developer of military napalm, designed the bat-sized incendiary bombs.
- 15 May 1943, Carlsbad Army Airfield auxiliary base, New Mexico: armed bats were accidentally released, roosted under a fuel tank and set the test range on fire. It destroyed buildings and the overseeing general's car.
- Passed to the Navy (Project X-Ray, Aug 1943), then the Marine Corps (Dec 1943); cancelled in 1944. About $2 million spent. Never used in the war.

## Narration (Kokoro am_michael)
1. In 1943, America built a bomb made of bats. Then the bats bombed America.
2. It started with a dentist named Lytle Adams, who visited a cave in New Mexico full of bats.
3. His idea: strap a tiny firebomb to every bat, and drop them over Japan, where many houses were made of wood and paper.
4. The bats would hide in the attics. Then the timers would go off.
5. Incredibly, President Roosevelt said yes.
6. The man who invented napalm designed bombs small enough for a bat to carry.
7. Then, during a test in New Mexico, some armed bats escaped.
8. And they did exactly what they were designed to do. They found the nearest dark hiding place. Under a fuel tank.
9. Luckily, the bat bomb worked perfectly. Unluckily, it worked on the army's own air base.
10. The fire burned down the test range, and even the general's car.
11. After two million dollars, the project was cancelled.
12. The bats never fought in the war. But they did win one battle.
