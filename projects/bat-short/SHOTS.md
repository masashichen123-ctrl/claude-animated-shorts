# The Bat Bomb: shot list (30 fps, TOTAL = 1650 f = 55.0 s)

- Voice: `voice/voice.wav` (Kokoro am_michael, 54.38 s).
- Word start frames: `voice/words.json` (seconds × 30). Lines: `voice/lines.json`.
- Picture events and SFX events are locked to the frames below. Visual reactions land 0-3 f before their word.
- XF = 5 f anchored dissolve. Everything else is a hard cut.
- Tone: playful cartoon. Nobody is hurt on screen. The "drop on Japan" beat is a TABLETOP MODEL on a planning table, never a real city.

Cast (our own designs, toon.ts Pen style as in the emu short):
- **Lytle Adams:** a cheerful 1940s dentist, round glasses, neat moustache, white dentist tunic or a tweed jacket with a bow tie, and a fedora for the cave visit.
- **The napalm chemist:** white lab coat, wild hair, safety goggles on his forehead.
- **The President:** our own cartoon: round glasses, a cigarette holder (no smoke), dark suit, at a desk.
- **The General:** peaked cap, big moustache, medals; he loves his shiny car.
- **Soldiers:** 1940s US Army khaki, garrison caps.
- **BATS are the stars:**
  - small and cute (Mexican free-tailed): brown-grey fur, big ears, tiny fangs, leathery wings;
  - a TINY bomb capsule strapped to the belly with a little ticking timer;
  - poses: fly cycle, hang upside down, peek, sniff, smug, wink;
  - swarms of 200+ cards must render fast.
