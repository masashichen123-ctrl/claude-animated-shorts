## What's working
1. **The premium look.** The two new-look Shorts got 4-20x the views of the old ones within 1-2 days, and about twice the like rate. Caveats:
   - the sample is tiny;
   - YouTube changed how Shorts views are counted on 16 Nov, which inflates newer numbers.

   Even so, the like rate is not affected by the counting change, and it doubled.
2. **Topic type.** True, absurd history with a cause and effect: a government or army doing something big and stupid, with a funny result. Emu War, missile mail, the time-locked vault and the exploding whale all fit. Gemini's "suspense/drama" read came from one video in a 3-video window.
3. **Title style.** Plain, confident, curiosity-gap statements:
   - "The Time Australia Went to War Against Emus"
   - "Robbers Took His Family Hostage. He STILL Couldn't Open the Vault."
4. **Hook.** The absurd fact in the first line, then a punch ("...against emus. And lost.").

## What's not working
1. **Inconsistent titles.**
   - The weakest video has emojis plus `#shorts` plus an exclamation mark ("...Still Sleeping! 🏨🤯 #shorts").
   - The missile title has `#animation #history #facts` in it.
   - Hashtags belong in the description, not the title.
2. **Covers don't show the subject.**
   - The whale video's cover shows a man with a camera and no whale.
   - The Chicago cover shows two random men.
   - The Robbers cover is a man in a nightcap.

   The cover matters on the channel page and in search.
3. **Comment counts are unavailable on all six.** Check in Studio whether comments are off, or whether the videos or channel are set to **"Made for kids"**:
   - Cartoon channels often get set that way by mistake.
   - It disables comments and notifications and limits reach.
   - The stories are general-audience history, so "No, not made for kids" is correct unless you deliberately target kids.
4. **Off-niche upload.** The fiction "Shadow" Short sits outside the channel's promise (true weird history) and has 0 views.
   - Check that it's public.
   - Consider keeping fiction off this channel.
5. **0 subscribers gained.** There's no reason to subscribe yet. Fixes:
   - a consistent series feel (same look, same narrator);
   - a steady schedule;
   - a one-line subscribe CTA plus a question in a pinned comment on every video.
6. **Irregular posting.** There was a 10-day gap (7-17 Nov), then 5 uploads in 4 days. 1 per day, or every other day, at a fixed time is better.
