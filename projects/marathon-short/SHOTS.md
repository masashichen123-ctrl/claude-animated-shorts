# The 1904 Olympic Marathon: shot list (30 fps, TOTAL = 1680 f = 56.0 s)

- Voice: `voice/voice.wav` (Kokoro am_michael, 55.7 s).
- Word start frames: `voice/words.json` (seconds × 30).
- Picture and SFX events are locked to the frames below. Visual reactions land 0-3 f before their word.
- XF = 5 f anchored dissolve. Everything else is a hard cut.
- Tone: playful cartoon. Nobody is hurt on screen beyond comic exhaustion. Poison is a small bottle with a skull-and-crossbones pictogram (no words).

Cast (our own designs, toon.ts Pen style as in the bat and whale shorts). All runners wear 1904 kit: sleeveless singlets, long baggy shorts to the knee, thin shoes, with a bib number shown as dots or stripes only, no digits.
- **Thomas Hicks:** tall, thin, moustache, red singlet. Fit at first, then grey-green, sweaty and wobbly with swirl eyes.
- **Fred Lorz:** fresh-faced, smug grin, white singlet, a cap.
- **The Cuban runner:** small and wiry, a white shirt with trousers cut off at the knee, a cheerful moustache.
- **Trainers/officials:** bowler hats, waistcoats, sleeve garters, goggles in the cars.
- **The 1904 crowd:** straw boaters, long dresses, parasols.
- **Dogs:** 2-3 scrappy cartoon farm dogs.
- **Props:** 1904 automobiles (open-top, brass headlamps, spoked wheels), a well with a bucket, an apple orchard, the stadium finish with a tape.
