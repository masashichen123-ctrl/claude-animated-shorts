# The Time Lock: shot list (30 fps, TOTAL = 1590 f = 53.0 s)

- Voice: `voice/voice.wav` (Kokoro am_michael, 52.6 s).
- Word start frames: `voice/words.json` (seconds × 30).
- Picture and SFX events are locked to the frames below. Visual reactions land 0-3 f before their word.
- XF = 5 f anchored dissolve. Everything else is a hard cut.
- Tone: playful cartoon heist-comedy. Nobody is hurt or threatened with a weapon on screen: the robbers are bumbling, wear bandana masks and flat caps, and "threaten" means shaking fists. No pistols. No words or digits anywhere.

Cast (our own designs, toon.ts Pen style as in the Chicago and marathon shorts), 1860s-70s clothing:
- **The banker:** round, nervous, waistcoat with a watch chain, sleeve garters, a big moustache; in a nightcap and long nightshirt when kidnapped. His knees knock.
- **Three robbers:** bandana masks, flat caps, striped shirts, one tall, one short and round, one with a huge moustache; clumsy and sneaky.
- **James Sargent:** a tinkerer with round goggles pushed up on his forehead, an apron full of tools, a thin moustache.
- **Townsfolk of Morrison, Illinois:** bonnets and bowlers.
- **The guard clock:** a cartoon wall clock with arms and legs, a little security cap, arms crossed; the film's mascot.
- **Props:** a huge round steel vault door (rivets, a big spoked wheel, a bolt), keys on a string, two kitchen wall clocks (brass-framed, pendulum), gears, a workbench, a crowbar, a rope, playing cards, knitting, cobwebs, a bird's nest, moonlit streets, a small prairie-town brick bank with a flag, a modern gleaming vault with a dial.
