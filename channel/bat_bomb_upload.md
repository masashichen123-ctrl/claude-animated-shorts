# Upload kit: The Bat Bomb

**Title:** America Built a Bomb Made of Bats. It Burned Down America's Own Base.
(Shorter alternative: The US Army's Bat Bomb Attacked the US Army)

**Description:**
In 1943, a dentist convinced the US military to strap tiny firebombs to bats. During a test in New Mexico, a few armed bats escaped, hid under a fuel tank, and burned down the army's own air base, including the general's car.

Sources: Wikipedia "Bat bomb"; Air & Space Forces Magazine "The Bat Bombers"; NPS "Bat Bombs and Balloons on Fire".
