In 1989, Pepsi got paid in Soviet submarines.
Pepsi had been selling cola in the Soviet Union for years, but Soviet money couldn't be spent outside the country.
So Pepsi was paid in things instead. Mostly vodka.
Then the deal got bigger.
Seventeen old submarines, a cruiser, a frigate and a destroyer.
On paper, a soft drink company now had warships.
Luckily, they never had to use them.
Unluckily, nobody at Pepsi knew how to sail a submarine.
So they sold the whole fleet for scrap.
Pepsi's boss went to the White House and told the president's security adviser:
"We're disarming the Soviet Union faster than you are."
You see, if your money only works in one shop, you get paid in whatever that shop has.
Sometimes that's vodka.
Sometimes it's a destroyer.
So the Cold War arms race had a surprise winner: the cola war.
