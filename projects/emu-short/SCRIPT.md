# The Great Emu War (1932) - script

## Facts (sources: Wikipedia "Emu War"; Britannica "Emu War"; Australian Geographic "Looking back: Australia's Emu Wars"; History Hit "The Great Emu War")
- After WWI, ex-soldiers (soldier settlers) were given farmland in Western Australia (Campion district), many growing wheat.
- Late 1932: about 20,000 emus migrated inland after breeding and ate and trampled the wheat; farmers asked the Minister of Defence for help.
- Major G. P. W. Meredith (Royal Australian Artillery) was sent with two soldiers (Sgt McMurray, Gunner O'Halloran), two Lewis machine guns and 10,000 rounds.
- 2 Nov 1932: the emus stayed out of range and scattered into small groups.
- 4 Nov: an ambush at a dam with about 1,000 emus; the gun jammed after about a dozen birds.
- Meredith mounted a gun on a truck; the truck couldn't keep up and the rough ground made aiming impossible.
- The operation ran 2 Nov - 10 Dec 1932 (with a break); Meredith claimed 986 kills using 9,860 rounds = exactly 10 rounds per bird.
- Emus run at about 50 km/h.
- Meredith: "If we had a military division with the bullet-carrying capacity of these birds it would face any army in the world. They can face machine guns with the invulnerability of tanks."
- The emus remained; farmers later used bounties and exclusion fences.

## Narration (Kokoro am_michael)
1. In 1932, Australia went to war against emus. And lost.
2. After World War One, veterans were given farms in Western Australia to grow wheat.
3. Then twenty thousand emus showed up, and ate it.
4. So the farmers called in the army.
5. Major Meredith arrived with two soldiers, two machine guns, and ten thousand bullets.
6. On the first day, the emus simply ran out of range.
7. Two days later, a thousand emus walked up to a dam, and the gun jammed.
8. So he bolted a gun onto a truck. The emus outran the truck.
9. After a month, he claimed nine hundred and eighty-six emus, with nine thousand, eight hundred and sixty bullets.
10. Luckily, the army had plenty of bullets. Unluckily, every emu took ten.
11. You see, a machine gun is made for an enemy that marches in a line. Emus run fifty kilometres an hour, in every direction at once.
12. Meredith said an army that could take bullets like these birds could face any army in the world.
13. The army went home. The emus are still there.
